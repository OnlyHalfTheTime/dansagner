### Can't relate.

It's "okay" social commentary, but it's a tad trite and told exclusively from the perspective of a gay writer from the 70's on the West coast, four things I'm not.  This doesn't bother me, and normally it doesn't stop me from enjoying the book.  However, it was managed in a heavy-handed way that left the reader wondering if he missed something.

Individual quotes show some insight, but much of it is "kids these days"-type rubbish.  I'll not be re-reading this one.
