### The only interesting thing about this book is how boring I found it.

I think this is more to do with its over-saturation in culture.  Cheshire Cat, the white rabbit, Tweedle-Dee/Dum, The animated movie, etc.  They are all cultural touchstones.  So, while it was interesting to read the book whence they came, it was more like reading a history book.  

Further, while it may have been novel for the time, the concept has become a trope.  So much so that The White Rabbit was famously Neo's breadcrumb to exiting The Matrix.  It's everywhere... The memes, not the Matrix.  Well, the Matrix, too.  Maybe Carroll *was* on to something.
