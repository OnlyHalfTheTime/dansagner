### I hate this book.  Everyone should read it. 

I gave this a 4/5 because scenes from it stick with me.  It's been almost 2 decades since my last read of it, and I can still see the rats.  I can imagine the awkward attempt at something akin to romance.  

That's what I took from my last read: Orwell's depiction of a forbidden love.  Not forbidden because of taboo, or by society, or by feuds, but by the state.  By force.  This *need* to love and be loved, to have some thing and some one that is yours and only yours.  That this cannot be successfully driven out of man.  That like the dandelion sprouting from the cracks in the pavement, is will grow wherever it is given the slightest chance.  And like that dandelion, Orwell does not shy away from showing us the heel that stamps it out. 

Dour, awful, terrible and terrifying.  There is a reason it's a meme.
