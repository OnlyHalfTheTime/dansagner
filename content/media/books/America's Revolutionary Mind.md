### Exceptional.

I need to go back and re-read this.  If my reading list weren't longer than my arm, I would be reading it now.  Thompson's arguments and historical facts work hand-in-hand to paint a picture of an entire *culture* ready for revolution, ready for freedom.

I remember being sad while reading this, alongside the Federalist Papers, at how our minds seem to have slipped year-over-year since the founding.  Go read any one of the Federalist Papers, then remember that this was intended for consumption by the common man.  People used to use *words* that meant *specific* things.  Perhaps we do not spend enough time in writing, in reading.
