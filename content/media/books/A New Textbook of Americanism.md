### A bunch of essays with which I already agree.

An Ayn Rand collection this is not.  None of the facts or arguments are *wrong*, but they are not as bombastic and well-presented as Rand and Peikoff.  I fear we shall never see their like again.  If this was the first Objectivist book I had picked up, I'm sure I would have had my interest piqued.  Alas, it is far from the first.  For this reason, I was just a bit bored.
