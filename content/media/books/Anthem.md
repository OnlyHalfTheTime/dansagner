### Ayn Rand's 1984.

I jest, but that's the shortest summary that gives you the gist.  This is not Mrs. Rand's best work, but that's still high praise.  

The sterile, rigid, mathematics-esque rubric around which the story proceeds is maddening to me, the sheer impossibility of such a society.  Along the same lines as my 1984 review, this is a love story first and foremost, about wanting something that is yours and yours alone.  I think that Rand speaks almost as well as Orwell in this regard.  There is something *very* human in the word "mine," as well as the Unspeakable Word.

My suspension of disbelief, however, has its limits.  There are just too many things that would not have actually worked.
