### You lookin' for a tilly, buddy?  Let's have a donnybrook!

Pump the brakes, super-chieftain.  Were you dropped as a child?  Cuz I'll drop you as an adult.  Anyone who hasn't watched Letterkenny is spare parts, bud.  That's all I can say.

Filled with enough niche Canadian/Hockey slang to drive you to drink maple syrup, Letterkenny is a fast-paced dialogue-heavy sitcom occassionally interrupted by brawls.  It is responsible for quite a few of my verbal tics and in-jokes, second only to Scrubs.  I can confidently say that if you watch the first episode and like it, you'll like the rest of the series.  Give it a shot.
